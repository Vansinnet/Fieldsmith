# Fieldsmith: Engine-Facing Contracts

Risk: **runtime-sensitive** (Harmony patches on placement and stamina, private placement state, object creation and destruction, UI and input).

Baseline: Valheim **1.0.20** (network version 41, Steam build 25909357), Unity 6000.0.75f1, BepInEx 5.4.23.5 (Valheim BepInEx pack), HarmonyX 2.9.0, as recorded in `docs/evidence/baseline.json`. Re-checked on 2026-10-15 by comparison with the archived 1.0.17 code (`reference/archive/1.0.17-25730771/decompiled`): 1.0.20 moves the ZDO cheat flag to the new internal `CheatUtils` (`IsCheated`/`SetCheated`, an int revision instead of a bool), bumps the save and network versions and changes drop and upgrade randomness. Of the members used here only the body of `Player.PlacePiece` changed: a cheated placement now marks the new ZDO through `SetCheated`. Fieldsmith passes the same `cheated` argument vanilla `TryPlacePiece` computes (`Inventory.ItemCheated`, `NoCostCheat`, `s_bypassCheatChecks`, all unchanged), so no code change was needed. Verified on 2026-10-08 against 1.0.17 and `reference/decompiled/assembly_valheim` and `assembly_utils` and `reference/assemblies/BepInEx.xml` / `0Harmony.xml`, and reviewed by an independent pass over the same sources, whose findings were addressed. `Localization` (assembly_guiutils) and `PlatformUserID` (Splatform) are verified by compilation only.

Clean-room design. FarmGridRemake 1.1.3 was named by the user as a feature reference; its files were not inspected (folder access was not granted) and none of its code was used.

## Design Summary

Fieldsmith only acts for the local player while a plant piece is selected: a `Piece` whose prefab has a `Plant` with `m_attachDistance <= 0` and none of the special placement flags listed under *Plants*, or an *object*: another placeable piece in the build menu of a planting tool (see *Objects From Other Mods*). After every vanilla ghost update it moves the ghost to the nearest cell of a square grid and replaces the position-dependent placement status with the status of that cell. A click plants an N x N block through vanilla `Player.PlacePiece`, one call per plant, so every plant is an ordinary piece created exactly as vanilla creates it. Vanilla `UpdatePlacement` then charges one plant; Fieldsmith charges the rest. Plants are ordinary networked objects; other players and the server need nothing installed. Fieldsmith also adds 25 of the game's wild plants (berry bushes, mushrooms, flowers, small trees, bushes, debris) to the Cultivator as objects (see *Wild Plants*).

## Objects From Other Mods

Objects are pieces without a `Plant` in a *planting tool*: a `PieceTable` with at least one handled plant (the Cultivator). They are learned from the table when a tool is selected (`Controller.Activate`), again once whenever a piece of the menu is selected that is not known yet (other mods change the menu while playing), and from every `ObjectDB.m_items` item's `m_shared.m_buildPieces` once per scene for harvesting (`Plants.RegisterAll`). Fieldsmith has no dependency on any of these mods.

### Plant Everything (studied integration, not referenced)

Plant Everything 1.21.3 by Advize, GUID `advize.PlantEverything`, `Advize_PlantEverything.dll` SHA-256 `313d57d5d9df2a27bff04ab184487d544a0749bbbe2c10cb1c41a0ae964cac62` (the user's Vortex copy, decompiled under `reference/mods/PlantEverything/1.21.3/` for investigation only; no code used). Observed in its code:

- Berry bushes, mushrooms, thistle, dandelion, smoke puff, fiddlehead, branch, stone, flint, small trees, bushes, shrubs, ygga shoot, ash fern, vines and configured extra resources are world prefabs with an added `Piece` (`m_groundOnly`/`m_groundPiece`, `m_cultivatedGroundOnly` optional, `m_onlyInBiome`) put into the Cultivator's `PieceTable` in `ZNetScene.Awake` and again after configuration changes. Fieldsmith handles them as objects.
- Its four custom saplings and the vanilla saplings it adjusts keep a `Plant` and are handled as plants.
- Its patches (`Piece.SetCreator`, `Piece.Awake`, `TreeBase.Awake`, `Plant.Grow`, `Plant.HaveRoof`, `Plant.HaveGrowSpace`, `Plant.GetHoverText`, `Pickable.GetHoverText`, `Pickable.Awake`, `Pickable.SetPicked`, `Player.RemovePiece`, `Player.CheckCanRemovePiece`, `Piece.DropResources`, `ZNetScene.Awake`, `ObjectDB.Awake`) do not touch placement, the ghost or stamina. Both mods postfix `Piece.SetCreator` and `Plant.GetHoverText` independently.
- Its `HaveRoof`/`HaveGrowSpace` overrides relax growing for saplings; Fieldsmith still skips cells by the vanilla rules (conservative).

## Wild Plants (Flora)

`Core/FloraCatalog.cs` lists 25 vanilla world prefabs and their costs; `Game/Flora.cs` makes them Cultivator pieces. The selection, the catalog costs (used with `[Plants] Costs = PlantEverything`; the default `Seed` prices pickables at one pick), removability, recovery, ground rule, vine snap points and the fiddlehead yield follow the **default configuration** of Plant Everything 1.21.3 (Advize, GPL-3.0, source https://github.com/AdvizeGH/Advize_ValheimMods, license confirmed 2026-10-15). Only these facts were taken; no code, text or asset (its custom icons, "_Picked" spawner visuals and four custom saplings are not used). The four custom saplings are left out on the user's decision: they are new networked prefabs that every client and the server would need. Prefab values: `docs/evidence/flora.json`; costs: automated test `FloraCostsMatchPlantEverythingDefaults`.

## Client, Host And Server

- **Client:** everything above. Settings are local and not enforced by any server.
- **Listen host:** as a client; other players' placements never reach the patches' handled paths (`player == Player.m_localPlayer`).
- **Dedicated server:** `Application.isBatchMode` returns before any patch is applied.
- **Vanilla and console clients:** see the plants as ordinary objects, including placed wild plants (vanilla prefabs; on their side without the added `Piece`, so they cannot remove them with the hammer and get vanilla yields when they own them).
