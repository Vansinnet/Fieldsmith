# Fieldsmith: Runtime Acceptance

Status: **Runtime-pending**. No Fieldsmith game session has been observed. General method, environments and log locations: `docs/testing.md`.

Record for every session: date, role (single player, listen host, client), Valheim and BepInEx version, plugin SHA-256, world (always a disposable copy), other installed mods (especially other planting mods) and relevant log lines. Add the result to the observation log at the end of this file.

## Smallest Required Test

1. Disable other planting mods in Vortex (FarmGrid / FarmGridRemake, MassFarming, PlantEasily and similar); they also move the plant ghost or replace planting. Deploy with `scripts/Deploy-Mod.ps1 -Mod Fieldsmith` and start the game. Confirm `Fieldsmith 0.1.0 loaded` in `BepInEx/LogOutput.log`, with no `[Error` lines from Fieldsmith.
2. On a copy of a world, cultivate a patch of ground, equip the Cultivator and select carrot (or any) seeds. Confirm the panel on the left and one line per plant in the log with its grow radius, collider reach and automatic spacing.
3. With 1x1, plant a row of single plants: each snaps to the grid one spacing apart. Then set 3x3 (Shift + wheel or F8) and click next to the row: nine plants appear, aligned with the row, nine seeds and 9 x `StaminaPerPlant` stamina (less with Farming skill) are used.
4. Wait until the plants have been in the ground for at least 10 seconds and hover each one: every plant must say healthy, none "needs more space". Press Backspace: the last nine plants disappear and nine seeds come back.

## Full Matrix

- Pattern sizes 1 to 10; even sizes (2x2, 4x4) surround the aim; the aimed cell is always planted first.
- Every Cultivator plant: carrot, turnip, onion, barley, flax, magecap, jotun puffs, and the saplings (beech, birch, oak, fir, pine, yggdrasil). For each: log line spacing, all plants healthy after growing starts, grown crops and grown neighbours do not block healthy growth. Record the logged spacings in `docs/evidence/`.
- Following rows: a hand-planted field at an angle; clicks continue its rows; near wild trees, bushes and rocks the grid does not anchor to them; F6 lock and unlock; Alt + wheel on a free, followed and locked grid.
- Skipped cells: uncultivated ground at the edge of the patch, a fence post or rock in the field, under a roof, in the wrong biome (barley outside the Plains), mountain cold, under water, inside another player's ward, in a no-build area. The message counts and names the reason; no seeds or stamina are charged for skipped cells.
- Limits: fewer seeds than cells; stamina lower than the cost of the block; `StaminaPerPlant = 0` (no stamina used); maximum value 25; `StaminaSkillReduction` off; world stamina modifier; free build and `nocost`.
- Durability: `DurabilityPerPlant` on and off; the Cultivator breaks mid-click.
- Skill: Farming rises once per plant; undo then plant again does not raise it again for the undone plants.
- Undo: several clicks in a row; after the plants have grown (nothing to undo); after walking away; with a full inventory (seeds dropped at the feet); after reconnecting (history cleared).
- Off-terrain aims: a plant on a wooden floor (vanilla single placement), aiming at a rock or a building; inside a dungeon and in the Deep North (grid off).
- Grid off (Y): vanilla planting, one at a time, stamina setting applied.
- Input: Shift/Ctrl/Alt + wheel never zooms while aiming at the ground; plain wheel still zooms; keys do nothing while chat, console, inventory, map or the build menu is open; remapped and unbound keys.
- Interface: panel and markers at 1080p and 4K, `PanelScale`, `ShowPanel = false`, `ShowPreview = false`, hidden HUD.
- Multiplayer on a listen host and a dedicated server: the planting client has Fieldsmith, the other client and the server do not; the other client sees every plant, they grow and survive a server restart. Undo while the other player stands next to the plants.
- Dedicated server with Fieldsmith installed: loads, logs that it does nothing, no errors.
- Block harvest: Left Shift + E on a ripe crop in a full field picks the block (pattern size, at least 3x3) and nothing outside it; unripe plants, wild berries and mushrooms are untouched; drops, Farming skill and bonus yield as vanilla; `StaminaPerCrop` limits the block; holding E does not repeat the block; another player owns the crops (multiplayer).
- Replant: with `Replant = true`, seeds and the Cultivator in the inventory, every harvested cell gets a new seed; without seeds or without the Cultivator nothing is planted and a message says so; a single Shift + E crop is replanted. Farming rises once per replanted seed (skill screen or `raiseskill` log), none after an undo debt.
- Grow timer: on a fresh carrot, a sapling (hours shown) and an unhealthy plant (orange, "(if healthy)"); the plant grows within about 10 s after the timer reaches zero; the timer is the same for another client looking at the same plant; `ShowGrowTimer = false` hides it.
- Together with Landsmith: both panels and key sets work, neither reacts while the other's tool is selected.
- Together with Plant Everything 1.21.3 (default configuration): the log lists every Plant Everything object once ("object without growth") with a plausible reach; raspberry, blueberry, mushrooms, thistle, dandelion, a small tree and a custom sapling (Ancient Sapling) snap to the grid and plant 3x3 blocks; no two objects overlap; cultivated-ground and biome rules match vanilla placement of the same object; the cultivate and replant-grass tools stay vanilla; rows follow placed bushes but never wild ones; Left Shift + E on a placed ripe bush picks the block, not wild bushes nearby, and nothing is replanted; undo returns the cost of unpicked objects and skips picked ones (also with `ResourcesSpawnEmpty = true`); enabling a Plant Everything category while playing makes its new pieces snap after selecting them; Plant Everything's own removal with the Cultivator still works.
- Wild plants without Plant Everything (`AddWildPlants = true`): the log says `Added 25 of 25 wild plants to the Cultivator.`; the Cultivator menu lists them after the seeds, each with its cost (raspberry bush 5 raspberries, mushrooms 5, fiddlehead 15, small beech 1 beech seed, bushes 2 or 3 wood, ygga shoot 1 yggdrasil wood + 2 wood, branch 5 wood, stone 1, flint 5) once the cost item is known; they snap to the grid and plant 3x3 blocks; vines place the vanilla way and snap to each other; a placed fiddlehead gives 3; the hammer removes a placed berry bush (no refund) and placed vines (wood back) but never a wild bush or mushroom; small trees and bushes cannot be removed; a vanilla client sees every placed plant; after a restart placed ones still give the catalog yield.
- Costs: with `Costs = Seed` the menu shows 1 mushroom, 1 raspberry, 3 fiddleheads, 1 wood for a branch, 1 flint, 1 stone; switching to `PlantEverything` while playing shows 5/5/15/5/5/1 on the next look at the menu; undo returns the current cost.
- Stone: a planted stone picked gives 1 stone, its model vanishes and a small one shows; after 4 h (or the growth setting) it is back; a picked planted stone survives a world reload; a wild stone picked is gone for good.
- Cultivator removal: middle mouse with the Cultivator on a planted mushroom, dandelion, branch, stone, raspberry bush (ripe: berries drop first), small beech and vines removes each one with the remove effect, stamina and tool wear; vines return 2 wood, nothing else returns its cost; a carrot, a sapling, a wild mushroom, a wild bush and a workbench are not removed; inside someone else's ward the ward flashes and nothing is removed; in a no-build area the message shows; after removing, the next plantings do not raise Farming until the debt is used up; with Plant Everything installed the Cultivator removes as Plant Everything does.
- Area removal: Left Shift + middle mouse with the Cultivator in a 5x5 mushroom field removes those within 3 m (count in the message), nearest first, also picked ones (small marker); carrots, saplings, wild bushes and buildings in the circle stay; with low stamina it stops and says so; plants inside someone else's ward are counted as protected; `RemoveRadius = 8` clears a larger field; with the hammer Left Shift + middle mouse still copies the aimed piece (vanilla); gamepad Alt + place with the Cultivator still copies.
- Wild plants with Plant Everything installed: the log says Plant Everything adds them and Fieldsmith adds none; no duplicate menu entries.
- `AddWildPlants = false`: no wild plants in the menu.
- Growth time: `GrowTimeHours = 0`, `GrowTimeMinutes = 2`: a fresh carrot and a sapling show "Fully grown in 2:00" and grow after about 2 minutes (half-grown model after 1); a planted raspberry bush picked shows "Grows back in 2:00" and returns within a minute after; set back to 0 h 0 min while playing: timers return to the game's times; a wild berry bush keeps 5 hours; multiplayer: another client owning the plants uses its own times. `GrowTimeHours = 1`, `GrowTimeMinutes = 55`: a fresh carrot shows "Fully grown in 1:55:00".
- Regrow timer: pick a planted mushroom and a planted raspberry bush; looking at the spot shows the name and "Grows back in" counting down (4:00:00 and 5:00:00 at the game's times); a wild picked berry bush shows it too; the object returns within about a minute after zero; the timer is the same on another client; after a world reload the planted mushroom spot is still hoverable; `ShowGrowTimer = false` hides it; unpicked ones still show the normal pick prompt. After picking, a planted mushroom, dandelion and branch show a small model (35 %) at the spot that disappears when the full one grows back; it is there after a world reload while still picked; wild picked ones vanish as in vanilla; another client without Fieldsmith sees nothing while picked.
- Remove the mod: plants remain and grow, no errors.

Until these observations exist, any explicitly requested release must remain a prerelease and cannot be marked stable/latest.
